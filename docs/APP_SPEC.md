# ChimeFlow Product Specification

## Product
- Name: ChimeFlow
- Application ID: com.prashantlatta.chimeflow
- Minimum Android: 10 / API 29
- Offline-first, no ads, no subscriptions, no mandatory internet.

## Languages
- English
- Hindi
- Vagdi in Devanagari, using Kherwara-region wording.
- Android TTS may not provide a native Vagdi voice. UI localization and TTS voice availability must be treated separately.

## Themes
- Follow System by default; manual Light and Dark options.
- Light background #F3EFE5, surface #F5F1E8, text #35342F, accent #A88D62.
- Dark background #000000, surface #080808, text #F2F0EB, accent #D6B56D.

## Navigation
- Dashboard
- Chimes
- Schedule
- Sound & Voice
- Settings

## Planned capabilities
- Multiple independent chime slots.
- Per-slot interval, sound, vibration, TTS, volume and active period.
- Daily and weekly schedules with multiple active ranges.
- Custom sound import.
- Custom vibration patterns.
- Speaking clock and custom announcements.
- Exact/inexact alarm handling and reboot/time-change rescheduling.
- Local versioned ZIP backup and restore.

## Non-goals
- No copying third-party proprietary code, sounds, graphics or branding.
- No promise of guaranteed exact playback on all Android devices.
- No cloud account or mandatory network connection.
